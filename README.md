# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis is a data analysis and machine learning project that studies agricultural performance across different seasons, crops, and regions.

The project analyzes factors such as rainfall, temperature, soil conditions, farming practices, crop yield, production, cost, revenue, profit, and water usage to identify important patterns and seasonal differences.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
* Compare crop yield between Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.
* Study the relationship between environmental factors and crop yield.
* Analyze production, revenue, cost, and profit.
* Identify important trends and patterns in agricultural data.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for better agricultural planning.
* Explore crop yield prediction using machine learning.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 agricultural records and 28 features** covering information related to:

* Farm and location details
* Crop and season
* Rainfall and temperature
* Soil conditions
* Fertilizer and pesticide usage
* Irrigation methods
* Crop yield and production
* Market price
* Cost, revenue, and profit
* Water usage and efficiency
* Disease and pest risk

## 📈 Key Findings

* Kharif recorded the highest average yield at **5.63 tonnes/ha**.
* Rabi recorded an average yield of **5.04 tonnes/ha**.
* Zaid recorded the lowest average yield at **4.64 tonnes/ha**.
* Kharif showed the strongest profitability among the three seasons.
* Higher seasonal rainfall was associated with higher average yield in this dataset.
* Seasonal differences were observed in rainfall, soil moisture, yield, revenue, cost, and profit.

## 🤖 Machine Learning

A **Random Forest Regression** model was implemented to explore agricultural yield prediction using the available agricultural features.

The analysis suggests focusing on:
